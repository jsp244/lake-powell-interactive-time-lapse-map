Imagery: Landsat Collection 2 (USGS) via Microsoft Planetary Computer — clearest June–October scenes each year, mosaicked at 75 m/px, scored for cloud over the lake itself. 1972–1983 use the early MSS sensor (60 m, no blue band: color is simulated, slightly rosier) — the lake was still filling until 1980. Water area = MNDWI water pixels (NDWI pre-1984) — narrow canyon arms are under-resolved, so treat it as a relative index. Elevation/storage: Bureau of Reclamation daily hydrodata, June–September mean. Full pool = 3,700 ft / 24.32 M acre-ft. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan. 2012 is Landsat 7 (striping).
